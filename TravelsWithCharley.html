AUG. 20 | Z. ZOLMER | S.R. LEE | CHAR. LEE

Travels with Charley in the P.N.W.

Sub. J. Steinbeck for p.r. guy, French poodle for p.w.d.


It is a shame to now see this beautiful little blog, which for one three-month period, three years ago, published deeply interesting articles on a topic of great importance, as now a personal diary or log, basically, of a single six-day roadtrip taken by a thirty-something couple and their dog.

What could be more unique? More urgent? Even the lowly Kings have more relevancy to you, the lowly reader.

But apparently I wanted to create this blog much more than I wanted to attend to it. And alas, this site, tho un-updated, still stayed up, these last threee years.

So what better place than here, now? The title and overall theme still apply. In some way. I'll factor that in.

First, here's the basic schedule or itinerary, which a better English major could easily distinguish btwn.

But also, it's going to lay everything out, and it could even update, day by day, as we go, sometimes thru wooded, barely populated areas, likely guilty of nothing in reality, but myth-ologized thru Deliverance in a very specific, scary way, cutting deeply to the white American male psyche (of which I am part), so you may share this link among loved ones as you please, but don't go getting me or S. or the dog hunted and killed, accidentally putting this beautiful, delicate site before the red-rimmed eyes of some drug- or Facebook-crazed criminal-type.
