AUG. 20 | Z. ZOLMER | S.R. LEE | CHAR. LEE

Travels with Charley in the P.N.W.

Just sub. J. Steinbeck for p.r. guy, French poodle for P.w.d.


What a shame to see this beautiful little blog, which for four months, three years ago, published deeply interesting articles on a topic of great importance, now home to an indulgent travelogue, of a six-day roadtrip taken by a thirty-something couple and their dog.

What could be more unique? More urgent? Even the lowly Kings have more relevancy to you, the lowly reader.

But apparently I wanted to create this blog much more than I wanted to care for it. And alas, this site, tho un-updated, still stayed up, these last threee years.

So what better place than here, now? The title and overall theme still apply. In some way. I'll factor that in.

But here's the basic schedule, or itinerary, whichever is better.

But also, this lays everything out, and could even update, day by day, as we go, thru long stretches of wooded, 'off-grid' areas, probably guilty of nothing in reality, but myth-ologized thru 'Deliverance' to great psychic effect, so you may share this link among loved ones as you please, but don't get me or S.R. or the dog hunted and killed, inadvertently putting this beautiful, fragile little site before the red-rimmed eyes of some drug- or Facebook-crazed criminal-type.
